PRISOSQA — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

1. Scope and Contact

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use PRISOSQA (the “App”), an iOS game offered by an independent developer (the “Developer”). The Developer can be contacted at bontshalex@gmail.com regarding privacy matters or support.

PRISOSQA does not require a separate account with the Developer. Certain features rely on services provided by Apple or Google, each of which also handles information under its own privacy terms.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App maintains information needed to operate the game, including scores, throwing and distance progress, personal records, available attempts, gameplay history, purchase and reward records, and sound or haptic preferences. The App may also retain local technical identifiers and state information needed to protect purchases, maintain consistency, and avoid granting the same reward more than once.

These records are primarily stored on your device using local app storage, including a local database and device settings. Certain app-related information may be kept in the iOS Keychain. The Developer does not operate an account database or a server that routinely receives your local gameplay history.

3. Apple Game Center

If you use Game Center, PRISOSQA communicates with Apple to authenticate your Game Center account, submit eligible personal-best scores, and display leaderboard results. Information used for these functions may include your Game Center player identifier, display name, score, and leaderboard rank. Your display name and eligible results may be visible to others through Game Center leaderboards, subject to Apple's settings.

Apple operates Game Center and controls information maintained by that service. Learn more in Apple's Game Center & Privacy notice.

4. In-App Purchases

PRISOSQA offers optional in-app purchases of additional game attempts through Apple's StoreKit system. Apple processes the payment. The App receives and processes the information needed to verify and deliver a purchase, such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, verification results, and transaction status. The App also maintains related local records to prevent duplicate delivery.

The Developer does not receive your payment card number or full payment credentials through the App. Apple's handling of transaction and payment information is described in App Store & Privacy.

5. Optional Rewarded Advertising

PRISOSQA uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded video advertisements. Watching an advertisement is a voluntary choice; the game does not require you to watch an ad to access its basic gameplay. When you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement and the advertising service confirms the reward, the App grants two additional attempts. Advertising may not be available for every user or every request.

To request, deliver, measure, and protect advertising, Google's Mobile Ads software and participating advertising providers may process information including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • Device characteristics and identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • Information about advertisements shown, interactions, and video views;
  • App and advertising performance information, diagnostic information, and fraud-prevention signals; and
  • Applicable privacy and consent choices.

The categories used in a particular case depend on the services provided, regional rules, device settings, and applicable permissions. Google and its advertising partners may process data under their own privacy terms. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The Developer does not operate a separate third-party analytics account or its own remote gameplay analytics service as part of PRISOSQA. This does not mean that Apple or Google collect no service-related usage or diagnostic information.

6. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, PRISOSQA uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present choices about advertising-related data processing. These choices may include consenting, declining consent, or managing individual purposes and advertising providers.

You may withdraw or change consent through the privacy options made available in the App where applicable. You can also use relevant iOS privacy controls, including controls for app tracking permission and advertising identifiers. A decision not to consent to personalized advertising does not prevent you from using the App's basic gameplay; however, it may affect whether an optional rewarded advertisement can be offered.

Permission to track across other companies' apps or websites, when applicable, is handled through Apple's separate App Tracking Transparency framework. Providing advertising consent does not automatically grant iOS tracking permission.

7. Purposes and Sharing

Information is handled for the purposes of running and saving gameplay, maintaining purchases and reward integrity, providing Game Center leaderboards, delivering optional advertisements, respecting privacy choices, securing services, and responding to support requests.

The App communicates with Apple for Game Center and in-app purchases, and with Google and participating advertising providers for advertising and consent management. The Developer does not sell a separate database of player profiles or gameplay history. Third-party service providers may have their own data practices as explained in their privacy notices.

8. Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay and preference data generally remains on your device while it is needed for the App to function. Deleting the App ordinarily removes its app-container data under iOS rules, but some items may persist outside that container, including Keychain items, Game Center records held by Apple, and records held by Apple or Google.

To remove locally stored gameplay information, you may delete the App and, where desired, use the applicable device settings. To manage information held by Apple or Google, use the privacy controls and request mechanisms offered by those services.

If you contact the Developer, correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage the request, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence or other personal information under the Developer's control by emailing bontshalex@gmail.com. Requests will be assessed under applicable law. The Developer cannot directly delete information independently controlled by Apple or Google.

9. Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the handling of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent where consent is used. You may contact the Developer at bontshalex@gmail.com to exercise rights concerning information under the Developer's control. You may need to contact Apple or Google directly for information those services control.

10. Children

PRISOSQA is not specifically directed to children. Advertising and information processing for users who are minors are subject to applicable laws, age-related device or account settings, and the privacy requirements of the advertising services involved. Parents or guardians with privacy concerns may contact the Developer using the email address below.

11. International Processing

Apple, Google, and their service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their respective privacy notices explain their data-processing locations, safeguards, and international transfer practices. The Developer's handling of support communications may also involve the email service used to receive and respond to your message.

12. Support Communications

When you email the Developer, the Developer receives your email address, the message, and any other information you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your inquiry, provide support, or address privacy requests; it is not used to create a gameplay advertising profile.

13. Hosting of This Policy

This page is hosted using GitHub Pages. Visiting the webpage is separate from using the App. GitHub may process technical information about visits to hosted pages under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

14. Changes

This policy may be updated when PRISOSQA's features, service providers, or data-handling practices change, or when legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy is revised.
