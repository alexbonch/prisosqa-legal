Game help for iPhone and iPad

PRISOSQA is a suction-cup throwing game. Aim for the target, build scoring streaks and advance from 1.0 m to the final 9.1 m mark.

How to Throw

Touch the suction cup, drag upward and release to throw. Your gesture controls POWER and direction. A valid upward gesture releases automatically at the upper release boundary.

  • Scoring hit: stick with the cup fully inside the target to earn points.
  • PREMIUM: stick while touching the red center for a higher base score. PREMIUM takes priority over a regular hit.
  • SAFE GRAZE: stick while touching the target edge without a scoring hit. No points, no distance gain and no attempt spent.
  • MISS: a miss, fall or glass hit outside the target costs one attempt.

SAFE GRAZE and MISS reset your streak. Scoring depends on the hit zone, distance, target shape and color, with additional streak, Perfect Meter and finish bonuses. The in-game HELP screen lists the scoring values.

Attempts and Progress

A new run starts at 1.0 m with 10 attempts. Each scoring hit advances DISTANCE by 0.1 m without spending an attempt. Reaching each full meter from 2.0 through 9.0 m earns one extra attempt.

NEW GAME starts a new run: SCORE returns to zero, and your current streak and progress reset. PERSONAL BEST stays saved.

Continue the Same Run

When attempts run out, OUT OF ATTEMPTS offers ways to continue:

  • CONTINUE GAME: purchase seven extra attempts at the price shown by the App Store.
  • WATCH AD: when the advertising feature and an advertisement are available, voluntarily watch a rewarded ad for two extra attempts after the advertising service confirms the reward.

Both options continue the same run without losing SCORE or DISTANCE. You can continue more than once. Cancelled payments and ad views without reward confirmation grant no attempts.

WORLD RANKING

Tap the trophy to open the single worldwide ALL TIME leaderboard through Game Center. It shows the TOP 10 and your individual world rank for your best score.

Online ranking requires Game Center access and an internet connection. An unavailable rank is shown as a dash. PERSONAL BEST stays saved on your device even without Game Center.

SOUND and HAPTICS

SOUND turns game music and sound effects on or off, including the stick, welcome and victory sounds.

HAPTICS turns gameplay vibration on or off on supported devices, including grab, hold and hit feedback. Both settings are saved.
